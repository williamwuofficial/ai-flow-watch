Signals

Range

1 · Broad passive flows

Rolling 20-day net creations/redemptions as % of fund assets. SPY & SPYM have full history; IVV, QQQ, QQQM are added as their snapshots accumulate.

2 · AI-theme flows

Tech, semiconductor and software ETFs (XLK with history; SMH, SOXX, IGV accumulating).

3 · Leveraged long tech & semis

TQQQ, QLD, USD, ROM (+ SOXL, TECL accumulating). Inflows during a drawdown are retail buying the dip; outflows are capitulation.

4 · Cap-weight vs equal-weight

Price ratio of cap-weighted to equal-weighted funds. Passive inflows push this up; a break below its averages means the biggest stocks are losing to the average stock.

5 · AI basket vs equal-weight market

Equal-weighted NVDA, MSFT, AVGO, AMD, TSM, GOOGL, AMZN, META divided by RSP, rebased to 1 on 2 Jan 2019.

6 · Flow price-impact

26-week rolling slope of weekly SPY returns on weekly broad passive flow (% of AUM). A rising slope means each dollar of flow moves prices more — the inelastic-markets mechanism. Correlation only: flows also chase returns.

7 · Asset-manager futures positioning

CFTC Traders in Financial Futures, average of E-mini S&P 500 and Nasdaq-100: net long as % of open interest. Asset managers (pensions, insurers, index funds) are slow, real money; hedge funds are fast money, often short against them. Weekly (Tuesday positions, published Friday). Dips around March/June/September/December expiries are contract rolls, not selling.

8 · Taiwan foreign investor flows

Rolling 20-day net buying by foreign investors on the Taiwan Stock Exchange, billions of TWD. Taiwan is the AI hardware supply chain (TSMC).

9 · Korea foreign investor flows

Rolling 20-day net buying by foreign investors on KOSPI, trillions of KRW. Korea is the AI memory supply chain (Samsung Electronics, SK Hynix).

Data & coverage

Flow = change in shares outstanding × NAV. "Snapshot" funds have no public history, so their record starts on the first day this site collected them. Estimated = shares implied from reported fund market cap.

Method & caveats

  • Traffic lights. Flows: green if the 20-day sum is positive, amber if negative but the 60-day sum is still positive, red if both are negative. Ratios: green above both the 50- and 200-day averages, amber below one, red below both. Positioning: weekly readings during quarterly contract rolls are skipped; it counts as weak when at least 1 standard deviation below its 52-week median and falling when down at least 3 points over 4 weeks: one of those is "weakening", both is "reversing". Historically that fired "reversing" in about 14% of weeks, clustered in 2016, 2018, March 2020, 2022 and early 2025.
  • Creations ≠ end-investor demand day to day. Market makers create and redeem for inventory, and "heartbeat" trades around index rebalances create and redeem billions within days. Creations over 2% of assets reversed by at least 80% within five days are netted out; share splits are ignored.
  • Much passive money is invisible. 401(k) target-date funds and collective trusts don't report daily. Vanguard (VOO, VTI, VGT) publishes no daily shares outstanding, so the largest S&P 500 fund is missing from daily flows.
  • Reporting lag. Shares outstanding usually reflect the previous day's orders; the impact regression shifts flows by one day.
  • Sources. State Street, ProShares, Direxion, iShares, Nasdaq, Cboe (delayed quotes), CFTC, FRED, TWSE, Naver Finance (KOSPI investor flows). Several are unofficial endpoints and may break; source health above shows the last successful fetch.
  • Not investment advice.
